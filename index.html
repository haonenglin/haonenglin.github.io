About Me

I am currently a PhD student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (supervisor: Prof. Harry Qin). My research focuses on vision-language models in the medical domain, particularly for tasks such as medical report generation, image segmentation, and other clinically relevant applications.

Prior to joining PolyU, I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Engineering from the School of Biomedical Engineering, Shenzhen University, in 2021.
